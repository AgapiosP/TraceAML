      Your workspace, at a glance.

      1. Choose a case

        Search your loaded queue or filter by status. Select a case to see its brief.

      2. Follow the evidence

        Findings show the observed facts, source movement, and supporting evidence ID.

      3. Review transactions

        Open a transaction, examine the source facts, and save a reviewed or escalated assessment.

      4. Record the outcome

        Add notes, manage assignment and status, and provide a human disposition before closing.

      Viewers can inspect and export. Analysts and admins can write. Closed cases are read-only. If another reviewer changes a case, reload before saving.
